The Problem Why the usual options don't fit

01

Cloud AI is off the table

CUI and classified data can't be sent to a commercial AI service. If the model runs outside the boundary, the conversation ends there.

02

Appliances arrive as a foreign object

A vendor box brings its own identity, networking, storage and update path. None of it matches what your ISSM has already accredited.

03

Pilots stall at integration

A model on one workstation is easy. Getting it behind your existing login, on the right segment, with an update process your team can follow is where projects stall.

The AI is the easy part. Making it work inside your boundary is the hard part, and that's what we've done for 25 years.
