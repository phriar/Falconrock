The Problem

Your Team Wants to Use AI.
Your Files Shouldn't Go With It.

AI tools are genuinely useful for finding, summarizing and drafting. The catch is where your information goes when someone pastes it in.

01

People Are Already Using It

Staff paste contracts, client emails and internal documents into free AI tools because it saves them time, whether or not there's a policy about it.

02

You Don't Control Where It Goes

Public AI services process what's typed into them on their own servers, under terms that vary by provider and plan, and that can change.

03

"Just Don't Use AI" Doesn't Work

Banning it leaves your team slower than competitors, and people tend to use it anyway. A private option gives them a safe place to do it.

For Your IT Person or Provider

We work alongside your existing IT provider, not instead of them. The system is built from open-source components: a local language model, a document index with retrieval (often called RAG) and a web interface, running on Linux, in containers or on a virtualization platform. It can run with no internet connection at all; if it's allowed to fetch updates, we document exactly what connects out and when. Specific components are chosen during discovery to fit your environment.

Straight Answers

Common Questions

Is it as good as ChatGPT?

Not at everything. The biggest cloud AI models are still stronger at open-ended writing and complex reasoning. For finding and summarizing information in your own documents, a well-set-up private model does the job well. The pilot is where you find out whether it's good enough for your work, before you spend more.

Does anything leave our office?

The AI and your document library run on your own computer, so your questions and files aren't sent to an AI provider. It can be set up with no internet connection at all. If you'd like it to download updates, we'll document exactly what connects out and when.

Does this make us compliant with HIPAA, or protect privilege?

No technology does that by itself. Keeping AI in-house removes one significant risk: sending sensitive information to an outside AI service. Compliance also depends on who has access, how the system is configured, and your own policies and procedures. We document exactly what we built so you and your advisors can assess it.

Will it learn from our data?

It reads the documents you give it so it can answer questions, but the AI model itself isn't retrained on your data. You decide which documents are included and can remove them.

Do we need an IT department?

No. Many small businesses have one IT person or an outside IT provider. We work alongside them and hand over documentation they can follow. We're not trying to replace your IT provider.

What does it cost?

There are three parts: the computer (you buy it; we tell you what to get), a fixed-price setup quoted in writing, and an optional support plan. Because it's built on open-source software, there's no per-user AI subscription. After a short discovery call we can give you real numbers for your situation.

Can different people see different documents?

In most setups, yes. For example, HR files can be kept separate from everything else. We confirm exactly what's possible for your situation during discovery.
