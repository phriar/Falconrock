The Problem

The Demand Is Real.
The Usual Options Don't Fit.

Teams in DoD and Intelligence Community environments want the same generative AI tools everyone else is using. The standard ways of getting them break down inside a cleared boundary.

01

Cloud AI Is Off the Table

CUI and classified data can't be sent to a commercial AI service. If the model runs outside the boundary, the conversation ends there.

02

Appliances Arrive as a Foreign Object

A vendor box brings its own identity model, its own networking, its own storage and its own update path. None of it matches what your ISSM has already accredited.

03

Pilots Stall at Integration

Getting a model running on one workstation is straightforward. Getting it behind your existing login, on the right network segment, with an update process your team can follow is where projects stall.

The AI is the easy part. Making it work inside your boundary is the hard part, and that's what we've done for 25 years.

How We Deliver

Five Steps,
Inside Your Boundary

We don't resell hardware or license a platform. We help you pick the right hardware, then integrate open-source components into the environment you already operate.

  1. 1

    Assess

    Agree on one use case, the users, the document set and the environment. Identify the constraints we have to work within, and write down what is in scope and what isn't.

  2. 2

    Size & Select Hardware

    Recommend GPU, memory and storage for the agreed use case and user count. You buy it through your normal channels. We don't resell hardware.

  3. 3

    Integrate

    Connect it to the services you already run, to the extent agreed in the assessment. Typically:

    • Sign-in through your existing directory or SSO
    • DNS and certificates from your existing services
    • Placement on the right network segment
    • GPU setup on your virtualization platform
  4. 4

    Deploy

    Install air-gapped from transferred media. Load open-source models and index the agreed document set so users can ask questions in plain language.

  5. 5

    Sustain

    Optional monthly retainer for a defined number of offline update bundles: newer model versions and updates to the deployed open-source components.
