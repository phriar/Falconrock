Capability Statement

Where the plan meets the site, we keep the build moving

FalconRock steps in at deployment on cleared and air-gapped data center builds. When licenses, hardware, approvals or upstream teams slip, we lay out the trade-off, get it approved within the existing ATO, and execute it on site.

What most teams say"We have to wait for X."
What we say"We can keep going. Here's what it will take."

Core Competencies

  • Dependency-slip recovery. Identify which stages aren't ready, what they block, and what can proceed now.
  • Interim workaround engineering. Temporary services built on the target design's names and VIPs, so cutover is a switch, not rework.
  • Compliance-aware pivots. Every workaround framed within RMF and the existing ATO, and briefed to the ISSM/AO before execution.
  • Rebuild-safe deployment. Bring-up structured so a failed attempt restarts fast, without re-transferring offline media.
  • Re-sequencing & runbooks. Revised implementation order and step-level runbooks teams can execute and auditors can follow.
  • On-site surge. We travel to the site and work extended stretches through critical build windows.

Pivots in Practice

DNS licensing late at go-live. Wait answer: hold the build. Ours: temporary DNS on the final DDI VIP. Build continued; no re-pointing at cutover.
Cloud foundation bring-up failed. Wait answer: reset and re-copy the depot. Ours: installer kept external to survive a rebuild. Fast restart, no new transfer.
Second data center not ready. Wait answer: delay the design. Ours: build primary now, designate the offline pair as future secondary.
